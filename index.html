The music I play is generally in the genre of post-psychedelic polka-jazz, and features mostly guitars and a laptop running Fedora with KDE Plasma. Both Linux and all the software I use are Free and Open Source, and includes Audacity, Sox, Kdenlive, OBStudio, Rosegarden, Qsynth/Fluidsynth, Hydrogen, and of course the Jack kit. I've also tried Rackarrack and Guitarix, but they both sounded like shit on my computer. So for audio processing I rely on either old fashioned amplifiers, or amplugs like Fender Mustang Micro+ or Blackstar Beam Solo. Or I go acoustic.
